Privacy policy

Last updated September 27, 2026. This covers the website and waiting list. The app has its own, shorter statement: it collects nothing.

What we collect
Your email address. Only if you submit the form and tick the box.
Why
To send you launch news. Nothing else.
Who processes it
Buttondown, our email list provider, based in the United States.
Cookies, analytics, pixels
None. This page loads nothing from anyone else.

What happens when you sign up

The form sends your email address to Buttondown. They store it so we can send you a launch email. Open and click tracking are turned off on our list.

The confirmation email

Right after you sign up, Buttondown sends you one email asking you to confirm. You are only on the list once you click the link in it. If you never click it, you get nothing else from us, and the unconfirmed address is deleted after 30 days.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or rent your address. We do not connect it to the app. The app has no account and sends nothing to us, so there is nothing to connect it to.

How long we keep it

Until launch, plus one year, or until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your email address at any time. Write to pp@neatnet.tech and we will delete it within 30 days. If you are in the EU, UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority. California residents have the same rights under the CCPA.
