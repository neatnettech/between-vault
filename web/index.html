What it is

A shared place for the things a couple needs to know: insurance numbers, doctor contacts, the boiler manual, the wifi password at your parents' house. Each of you keeps your own vault on your own phone, and you decide, item by item, what your partner gets.

No server in between. AirDrop, Messages or Files only carry the sealed file.

Why it is different

  • No cloud storage. Your vault is stored on your device. The app never uploads it.
  • No automatic synchronization. You control when information is shared.
  • No account. No email, phone number or sign in inside the app.
  • End to end encrypted exchange between two paired phones.
  • Your partner can recover your vault by design. Pairing is also your backup plan.

The honest limit. If both phones are lost and there is no backup, your data is gone. We cannot restore it, because we never had it.

Questions

Is there really no server?

Yes. The app has no backend. Items travel as an encrypted file you send yourself, through AirDrop, Messages or Files.

What if I lose my phone?

Install the app on your new phone, pair with your partner again, and they send you your recovery file. If both phones are lost with no backup, the data is gone.

Why open source?

Because "trust us" is not a security model. You, or someone you trust, can read every line.
